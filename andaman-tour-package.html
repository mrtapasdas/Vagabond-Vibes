PREMIUM PACKAGE

Andaman Paradise

4 Nights / 5 Days of Turquoise Waters and Pristine Beaches

Tour Overview

Escape to the exotic Andaman and Nicobar Islands with Vagabond Vibes. This 5-day premium getaway is crafted for those who want to blend historical exploration with pure tropical relaxation. From the moving history of Port Blair to the breathtaking, world-renowned coral reefs of Havelock Island, we handle all the logistics so you can focus on making memories.

Radhanagar Beach, Havelock
Scuba Diving at Elephant Beach
Historic Cellular Jail, Port Blair

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival & Historic Port Blair

Welcome to the Andaman Islands! Our representative will receive you at Veer Savarkar Airport and escort you to your premium hotel. After lunch and relaxation, we visit the historic Cellular Jail. In the evening, witness the stirring Light & Sound Show that brings the history of the Indian freedom struggle alive.

Day 2: Cruise to Havelock & Radhanagar Beach

After an early breakfast, board a luxury cruise to Havelock Island. Check into your beachside resort. In the afternoon, we head to Radhanagar Beach (Time Magazine's best beach in Asia) to experience silky white sands, turquoise waters, and a mesmerizing sunset.

Day 3: Elephant Beach Water Sports

A day for adventure! Take a speedboat to Elephant Beach, famous for its vibrant coral reefs. Enjoy complimentary snorkeling. You can also opt for Scuba Diving, Sea Walking, or Jet Skiing (extra cost). Return to the hotel in the afternoon for leisure time.

Day 4: Kalapathar Sunrise & Return to Port Blair

Wake up early for a drive to Kalapathar Beach to witness a beautiful sunrise against black rocks and blue waters. After breakfast, board the return ferry to Port Blair. Spend the evening shopping for souvenirs at the local Aberdeen Bazaar.

Day 5: Departure

After breakfast, check out from the hotel. We will drop you at the airport with unforgettable memories of your Vagabond Vibes tropical vacation. Safe travels!

Need to Know

Ferry timings are subject to weather conditions. Always carry a valid photo ID (Aadhar/Passport). Port Blair flights usually land in the morning and depart in the afternoon.
